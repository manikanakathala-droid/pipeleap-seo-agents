## What the data changed

- **Our own vocabulary isn't how buyers search.** "Sales stack optimization", "reduce sales admin", "too many sales tools", "sales tool sprawl", "selling time vs admin time", "unified sales inbox" and "sales operations transformation" returned **no autocomplete suggestions** in any market. Use them in copy, but target the phrases buyers do use: "how much time do sales reps spend selling", "non selling activities", "how to measure sales rep productivity", "sales tech stack", "sales team performance dashboard".
- **"Pipeline" and "velocity" are ambiguous.** "increase pipeline" suggestions are dominated by video games (CFB 26, Factorio); "sales velocity" variants are Amazon, CPG and real estate. Always qualify with "sales" and B2B context.
- **Coaching-framed results** dominate rep-performance searches (as expected). Target the diagnosis and visibility angle only.

- **Autocomplete gives the same answer per country** for most seeds, so it says little about regional differences.
- **Difficulty is judged from who ranks**, not a measured score.
