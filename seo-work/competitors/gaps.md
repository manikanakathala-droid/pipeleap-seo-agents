# Gap analysis, 2026-11-20

Sources: web searches on 2026-11-20 for the top clusters (US results). Competitor profiles below are from search-result snippets and need confirming on each vendor's own site before any comparison page is written (guardrails). Paid-tool backlink data: none available.

## Who owns the results

| Cluster | Who ranks now | Format | What they miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Admin vs selling time** ("how much time do sales reps spend selling") | Vendor blogs: Backstory, Mixmax, Salesmotion, Landbase, Skynamo, InsideSales, Prialto, Abstrakt; consultancies: Umbrex; press (Personnel Today, CBS) | Stat round-ups that repeat the same "~28–30% (Salesforce State of Sales)" figure, often unsourced or second-hand | A clear explanation of *why* figures conflict (definitions of "selling" differ), a method a manager can run on their own team, and the link from lost selling time to conversations and pipeline. Most stop at the statistic |
| **Selling-time calculators** | Skipcall (selling time calculator), Attio (CRM ROI calculator), Vablet, ClickUp (sales productivity calculator) | Cost-of-time calculators: hours × hourly cost | None connects time recovered to **conversations and pipeline** (the point of view). None separates measured vs estimated inputs |
| **Sales productivity software for small B2B teams** | Listicles: Trellus, Forecastio, CloudTalk, Snov.io, Apollo, Close, CRO Club, G2, Fitgap | "Best X tools" lists of CRMs and engagement tools | All assume buying another tool. Nobody argues "get more from the tools you already own" |
| **Sales tech stack consolidation / too many tools** | DealHub, SiftHub, Revenue Grid, Apollo, Subskribe, Outreach, Salesforce, TrustRadius, Revenue.io, Mindtickle | Vendor guides, mostly "move to our all-in-one platform" | The alternative to migration: keep the stack, remove the work between tools. One source even notes consolidation doesn't fix coordination between tools — that's Pipeleap's angle |
| **Which reps need help / underperforming reps** | Allego, Kixie, Sales Readiness Group, SBI, Sales Gravy, Everstage, ATD, Revenue.io, SyncGTM, Inc., Dummies | Coaching-led advice | Mostly framed as coaching; little on the data a manager needs to *see* first (selling time, overdue work, stalled deals per rep). Pipeleap fits as visibility, not coaching |
| **Sales velocity** | Outreach, Highspot, Pipedrive, Salesmate, Momentum, NetHunt, Demandbase, Drift, Saber, Precision | Definition + formula + generic levers | The time lever: more conversations from the same team (opportunities) and a shorter cycle from follow-ups that don't slip |
| **AI sales assistant on top of the CRM** | ZoomInfo, Momentum, Salesforce, Salesmate, monday, Guideflow, G2, Wonderchat, Arahi | Buyer's guides and listicles | Rep approval of everything, bring-your-own AI key, measured selling time |
